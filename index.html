YOUVAULT

YouVault (Vault) is a personal Android TV app. It shows the newest videos from the YouTube channels you subscribe to, so you can keep up with their updates on your TV.

Privacy policy

  • What Vault reads: with read-only access you approve (youtube.readonly), Vault reads your list of subscriptions, the names of those channels, and the titles, dates, durations and thumbnails of their newest videos, plus your channel name to show who is signed in.
  • Where it is kept: only on your TV, in the app's private storage. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with a key that never leaves the TV.
  • What is shared: nothing. Vault has no server, no analytics and no advertising. It connects only to Google and YouTube services.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall Vault, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Vault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
